THE ADVENTURE - for World of Warcraft: Forever, with a PS5 DualSense controller
==============================================================================

INSTALL
  1. Unzip this folder anywhere (your Downloads folder is fine).
  2. Double-click Install.bat and answer its questions. It:
       - puts the addon in World of Warcraft: Forever (Interface\AddOns\TheAdventure)
       - puts the DualSense helper in your user folder (%LOCALAPPDATA%\TheAdventure\Helper)
       - adds a Start menu shortcut, and (if you say yes) starts the helper with Windows
       - offers to download the speech model for talk to type (about 480 MB, once)
  3. Fully restart WoW (not just /reload) the first time.
  If Windows says the AddOns folder can't be written: right-click Install.bat > Run as administrator.

THE TWO PARTS
  The addon     the quiet HUD, fading chat, styles, quest journal, settings (type /adv). Works on its own.
  The helper    a small program for the DualSense over a USB cable: HD vibration textures and weather, the white
                health dots, the PS5 mic button for talk to type, the touchpad as a mouse in menus, the chat
                channel wheel, the adaptive trigger. Bluetooth works too, with the controller's ordinary rumble.

FOR THE HELPER
  - Plug the DualSense in with a USB cable for the full feel.
  - Quit Steam while you play (or turn off Steam > Settings > Controller > PlayStation Controller Support):
    Steam takes over the DualSense and the HD vibration and health dots stop working.
  - Play in windowed or borderless window mode (the helper reads a small strip of squares the addon draws in
    the top-left corner of the game to know what is happening).
  - Talk to type: click the PS5 mic button to open chat, hold it while you speak, let go and the words are typed.
    If the controller's mic sends no sound, turn the controller fully off (unplug it, hold PS for 10 seconds)
    and plug it back in.

PRIVACY
  Speech is turned into text on your PC. Nothing you say, and nothing from the game, is sent anywhere. The only
  download is the speech model, and only if you say yes. Recordings are not kept.

GOOD TO KNOW
  The helper reads the addon's squares on screen and presses keys for you only when you ask it to (the chat
  channel you pick, the words you speak). It never casts, attacks or hooks a fish for you. Blizzard hasn't said
  anything about tools like this; use it at your own judgement.

REMOVE
  Double-click Uninstall.bat.
